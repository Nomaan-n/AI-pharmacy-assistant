# Product safety contract

AI Pharmacy Assistant is an educational medication-information and organization tool. It is not a substitute for a pharmacist, prescriber, emergency service, or clinical assessment.

Verification states: `not_verified`, `candidate`, `candidate_verified`. An RxNorm match does not verify physical package identity, prescription authenticity, dose correctness, patient-specific suitability, or treatment decisions.

AI rules: never invent medication facts; interaction conclusions come from authoritative sources rather than the language model; OCR/handwriting creates candidates only; emergencies and dangerous symptoms escalate; start/stop/change-dose requests do not receive prescribing instructions; show source and verification status.

Before marketing clinical decision support or medical-device functionality, perform a jurisdiction-specific regulatory assessment. FDA's January 2026 CDS guidance distinguishes non-device CDS from functions that may remain medical devices, including some image-analysis and patient-specific decision-support functions. This is product policy, not legal advice.
